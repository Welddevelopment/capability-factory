# Frozen protocol

## Participant

A product/platform engineer who has not seen Capability Factory’s onboarding implementation or this workflow. Record prior familiarity before starting.

## Clock

Maximum elapsed window: eight hours. Record both elapsed time and participant-reported active time. Pauses must include a reason. The participant—not Joel and not an automation—starts and stops the clock.

## Allowed material

Only the participant bundle, the frozen product checkout, local package dependencies, and explicitly listed product documentation. No internet research and no customer or production data. Disposable secrets are generated locally and never written to evidence.

## No-author-help rule

Joel and implementation authors do not answer product or domain questions during the run. Questions go to an independent coordinator, who may return only a precommitted answer from the sealed answer key. Missing answers remain unknown and must fail closed.

## Required milestones

Record timestamps for: environment ready; proposal produced; operations reviewed; authority reviewed; observer compiling; first autonomous acquisition; first independently verified result; original goal resumed; fresh-process reuse; ten-case acceptance complete; final submission.

## Required outcome

The exact business result in `participant/input/outcome-and-collateral.md` must pass through an observer independent from execution. All ten acceptance cases must execute in the precommitted order. Zero unauthorized authority, invented credentials, blind retries, secret leaks, and surviving incorrect side effects are hard gates.

## Fair comparison

For a manual comparison, use a different fresh engineer or a counterbalanced second API. Do not have the same person complete Capability Factory and then manually implement the same API. The manual arm must meet identical safety and evidence requirements.

  environments, policies, and single-goal Playground paths remain present.

## Deterministic fixture behavior

The synthetic order-operations fixture contains seven required work items across six
reasoned groups:

- one order already satisfies trusted completion criteria;
- one order-detail task uses an existing configured ability;
- two independent inventory items share a retained North Supply capability;
- one East Industrial item uses the deterministic unsupported-residual acquisition path;
- one regulated-supplier item stops before execution for per-action approval in the
  partial-authority scenario; and
- one order-finalization item waits for and follows a directly verified stock dependency.

The partial scenario satisfies six items and leaves one precise zero-write approval
handoff. The complete-authority scenario produces a separate passing aggregate receipt.
These are fictional deterministic paths, not ERPNext, model, customer, or campaign runs.

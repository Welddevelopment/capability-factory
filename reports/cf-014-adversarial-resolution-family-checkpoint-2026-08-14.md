non-enumerable `evidenceId` because the compiler indexed the field but then
strictly parsed the smaller descriptor shape.

`evidenceId` an ordinary enumerable, strictly validated provenance field, and
the compiler parses that complete reusable contract. A focused regression
compiles a trusted-evidence graph with no hidden property.

- generic idempotent SQLite record upsert;
- generic schema-selected exclusive file transform; and
- generic verified-branch SQLite merge.

The bridge audit rejects case-specific source/callback keys and turns incomplete
file-transform or merge declarations into exact blockers. Focused negative
tests exercised both paths.

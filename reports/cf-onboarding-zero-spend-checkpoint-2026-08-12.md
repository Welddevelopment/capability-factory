# Capability Factory onboarding-gap checkpoint

Date: **2026-08-12**
Status: **deterministic local foundation implemented; under-one-day onboarding not yet proved**

## Outcome

Capability Factory now has a joined, fail-closed foundation for turning approved local system material and one bounded blocked workflow into:

1. a provenance-labelled adapter proposal;
2. a provisional independent-outcome verifier contract;
3. a draft authority contract compiled from ordinary confirmed answers;
4. a framework-neutral blocked-context envelope;
5. the fixed ten-case acceptance-plan scaffold; and
6. a seven-stage assisted-onboarding projection in the private console.

None of these generated artifacts silently becomes executable, authorized, passed, or activated.

## What still requires the customer, administrator, or engineer

The customer/domain owner still confirms the workflow, business success, collateral constraints, and documentation. The workspace administrator still confirms targets, permissions, approvals, limits, forbidden actions, and handoff owners. The platform engineer still supplies customer-local secret values, implements genuinely unsupported runtime or observation code, binds the trusted freshness gate, creates or confirms disposable reset, connects the upstream agent, executes the ten cases, and reviews evidence before explicit activation.

## Exact blocker to repeatable under-one-day onboarding

The largest blocker is no longer basic metadata extraction. It is the **safe generation and binding of a truly independent customer-specific outcome observer**, including freshness and collateral-state enforcement, followed by executable acceptance evidence. The current system can propose this contract but deliberately cannot make it pass itself.

The second blocker is empirical: no fresh platform engineer has yet completed the frozen unfamiliar-system onboarding task against a manual-integration baseline. Therefore the product cannot yet claim that setup takes less than one working day.

## Claim update

Existing current claim remains:

> Inside a predefined trusted scope, Capability Factory can let an agent encounter a missing HTTP capability, construct it from trusted documentation, test it, use it within explicit authority, verify the external result, resume its original goal, and retain the capability.

Newly supported private product statement:

> Given approved local OpenAPI, MCP, or SDK/reference metadata and a confirmed bounded workflow, Capability Factory can now deterministically produce a provenance-labelled, non-executable adapter proposal; scaffold authority, verifier, and acceptance contracts; and identify the exact remaining customer and engineering blockers without granting write authority or treating generated tests as evidence.

This does not yet support “automatic one-day onboarding,” “no integration work,” or “production-ready adapter generation.”
